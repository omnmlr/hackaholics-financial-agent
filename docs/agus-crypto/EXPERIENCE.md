# Experience direction: Android product and website

**Stage:** Product direction only. Do not start UI redesign before the technical gate and team planning.

## Android product
- The core AGUS Crypto experience is an **Android app**, not the normal wallet experience replicated in a browser.
- A calm, premium, coherent experience: account overview + natural conversation/voice + contextual financial actions + wallet approval + verified receipt.
- Voice commands should sound ordinary, not scripted multi-paragraph prompts.
- Support images and PDFs as natural context for questions, with untrusted content isolated from financial authorization.
- Relevant warnings should appear proactively where monitoring can substantiate them.
- Retain clear disclosure of watch-only accounts, authorized accounts, unsupported operations and required wallet app approval.

## Public website: product showcase + walkthrough + downloads
- Founder references: **HeyElsa** (crypto AI product/marketing site) and **Atlas Computers** (`atlascomputers.ai`), for their premium visual storytelling and polished feel. These are inspiration, not instructions to copy protected material.
- Target: unusually polished responsive experience on **desktop and mobile**, deliberate typography, animation/motion and high craft; consistent with AGUS's eventual mobile brand.
- A **single website** can combine hero, product narrative, interactive walkthrough/live mock demonstration, security/custody explanation, comparison/FAQ, roadmap, and app download destinations.
- This same walkthrough may support the live pitch, followed by a **real Android demo**; official hackathon submission requirements must still be met separately.
- Call to action: Android install/download link only when real artifact is published. Google Play and Apple App Store badges may say **Coming soon** until listings exist. No misleading store links.
- Web-only authentication, recovery, linking, redirected payments or other operational web flows should be added as **routes or subdomains of the same product** when actually required. No automatic requirement to build a second website.
- Site architecture and product app should have a shared visual language but distinct responsibilities.

## Design collaboration
Chisom is a proposed frontend and marketing-site lead; the founder will supply creative direction and review decisions. Both will plan the site and major animations together. Final ownership, design tools, and visual brief to be agreed in a meeting.

## Deferred choices
Specific layout, motion, fonts, 3D usage, color-system refinements, interactions, site framework and final copy. Don't overbuild a marketing experience at the expense of a functioning wallet-control demonstration.
