# AGUS Pocket feasibility

**Decision.** Pocket is technically credible as an optional, separately funded Swig account. Keep the external wallet and invoice workflow usable before Pocket is activated. The local experiment proves on-chain ownership, funding, spending and exit. Shipping in-app signing requires a separate Android acceptance run.

## Create, fund, spend and exit

- **Create.** Generate the configured Pocket address and show its chain and recovery method. For the earliest pilot, the user keeps a linked external wallet as root. Persist the actual configuration and vault addresses only after verifying creation and root ownership. Before funding, require a verified recovery route independent of the app install. For the longer-term default, evaluate an on-chain P-256 authority backed by Android passkey or a native user-presence signer, with a separate user-controlled recovery authority. The external root or recovery authority must be able to revoke device roles and withdraw while the AGUS service is down. Losing every enrolled authority can make assets inaccessible. A device biometric unlock alone does not establish that transaction signing is cryptographically gated. [Swig account creation](https://build.onswig.com/solana/create-wallet.md) · [P-256 authorities](https://build.onswig.com/solana/authorities/secp256r1.md) · [Recovery](https://build.onswig.com/solana/use-cases/recovery.md)
- **Fund.** Show verified network, source wallet and available eligible balance, Pocket address, amount, fee payer, network fee and any token account rent. Send SOL directly to the verified Swig vault. For classic SPL, use its ATA for the exact mint and verify the token authority. An idempotent ATA creation plus transfer can be one transaction. Use one explicit external wallet authorization per single funding transaction, after establishing the wallet connection. Connection, unlock and renewed consent can add prompts. Sending funds into Pocket requires a signature or a supported pre-existing token grant. For the launch path, use the wallet-signed transfer. [MWA signing](https://github.com/solana-mobile/mobile-wallet-adapter/blob/main/spec/spec.md#sign_and_send_transactions) · [Swig transfers](https://build.onswig.com/solana/use-cases/transfers.md) · [Local ownership and funding evidence](POCKET_PROOF_OF_CONCEPT.md)
- **Make a payment.** Require review of the resolved recipient, mint, amount, Pocket source, fees and expiry. A supported local/passkey authority or a user-controlled embedded signer can approve a decoded message inside AGUS or via a system authentication sheet. Such a signer would remove Phantom or Solflare from the ordinary Pocket payment path. A Pocket rooted solely in an external wallet still needs that wallet for root transactions. An app/device signer should hold a bounded role, separate from any server automation key. Keep new recipients, role changes and withdrawals behind the authority that actually controls them. [Swig signing](https://build.onswig.com/solana/transactions/signing.md) · [Android biometric-backed keys](https://developer.android.com/identity/sign-in/biometric-auth)
- **Automate.** Limit the first permission to one mint, approved destination token account, policy budget and session expiry, with explicit permitted programs and separate fee payer. “Set aside 20 USDC for weekly payments” can mean a funded budget or a cumulative recurring window. Swig's limits are counted in slots, so they do not establish a calendar week. The scheduler supplies calendar timing. A single 6-token transfer passed against an on-chain 20-token window. A fixed 5-token action is a remaining total. When combined with a recurring budget, it still gives a lifetime ceiling, as the source charges both actions. A hard 5-token maximum on every transaction under a reusable 20-token window needs a separately verified policy implementation. Offer individually approved payments, an explicitly capped one-off authorization or a clearly disclosed window cap in the meantime. A backend max can fail closed in the service and must be labeled application-enforced. A permission is distinct from reserving funds. Avoid a lock or escrow for ordinary transfers. [Swig spending limits](https://build.onswig.com/solana/permissions/spending-limits.md) · [Limit enforcement source](https://github.com/anagrambuild/swig-wallet/blob/c64127837995818ef5d1e161bbc367391a1b5765/program/src/actions/sign_v2.rs) · [Swig authorization locks](https://build.onswig.com/solana/permissions/authorization-locks.md)
- **Review and withdraw.** Show the verified vault balance, each role's scope and expiry, pending transfers and network fees. Pause the worker immediately on user request. Mark permissions revoked only after verified on-chain removal. Revoke delegated roles before a full exit when practical, reconcile pending operations, and sign a withdrawal to a user-selected verified destination. Show account rent separately. Returning the spendable funds and recovering all account rent through closure are separate flows. [Role removal](https://build.onswig.com/solana/remove-role.md) · [Local withdrawal result](POCKET_PROOF_OF_CONCEPT.md)

## Shortest safe Android flows

Counts are planned user actions from the relevant screen, excluding typing and optional wallet unlock or MFA. External launch means a wallet-controlled activity or sheet. Its appearance, unlock behavior and return are device-dependent.

| Task | Shortest proposed flow | Actions and boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Connect external | Choose wallet → approve address/network → return | Two decisions. Android opens the selected wallet. Offer paste-address watch-only as a separate path. |
| Activate Pocket | Enable → review root and recovery → authorize creation | Two to three decisions, one external handoff for external-root pilot. A passkey or embedded signer may use a system sheet after compatibility is proven. Deposit stays a separate action. |
| Fund Pocket | Select linked source, asset and amount → review → authorize in external wallet | One AGUS review and one wallet consent for the resulting transaction when a grant is reusable. Choices and unlock add actions. |
| Pocket payment | Say or enter payment → review exact details → approve with Pocket signer | One review and one user-presence prompt if configured. No wallet launch with a validated in-app signer. An external-root operation still requires its wallet. |
| External payment | Say or enter payment → review payer and details → approve in wallet | One review and one wallet consent. Same account/session checks as funding. |
| Grant recurrence | Review recipient, exact caps, duration, schedule and revocation → sign grant | One review and one root or external signature ceremony. In-place merchant plan and Pocket role are different authorizations. |
| Manage or revoke | Open Permissions → select grant → confirm/sign | Three actions. Step-up where available. Show “Paused in AGUS” until chain evidence permits “Revoked.” |
| Withdraw | Select amount and destination → review → root signs | Two to three decisions. Show pending and spendable balances separately and show any rent still held. |

Apply the verified Android package and grant to the exact signer. Prefer supported endpoint-specific association where available. PR #46's package-targeted bridge is tied to MWA 3.0.2 and adds a maintenance obligation. Vendor deep links are optional, separately tested transports. Validate the universal/App Link origin and bind callbacks to the pending request. Phantom's deep-link sign-and-send is deprecated. Solflare documents sign-and-send. AGUS cannot force an external wallet to stay in a compact sheet, skip an unlock or grant Auto-Confirm. Use a warm association where the wallet supports it, serialize handoffs, reopen the payment state on return and recover it on restart. [AGUS wallet compatibility](https://github.com/omnmlr/hackaholics-financial-agent/blob/4c132b7fb540a8f7b970ec4ff0873aa6ac44df7a/docs/AGUS_WALLET_COMPATIBILITY.md) · [Phantom deep links](https://docs.phantom.com/phantom-deeplinks/provider-methods) · [Solflare sign-and-send](https://docs.solflare.com/solflare/technical/deeplinks/provider-methods/signandsendtransaction) · [Android verified App Links](https://developer.android.com/training/app-links/verify-applinks)

PR #47 reported Phantom force-close and an identity warning, and Solflare explicitly blocked signing on the tested device in Low Power Mode. That establishes a wallet/version observation. Android can defer app network and background work under Doze. Keep settlement and reminders server-side, and test Battery Saver on and off, warm and cold wallet launches, return, lock screen, process death and offline restart. If a wallet blocks signing, show the actual restriction and retain the draft. Never automatically resubmit after a crash or lost callback. [Device journal](https://github.com/omnmlr/hackaholics-financial-agent/blob/87be10e5e9f4606e0c902f67f488a9c8f1823869/docs/agus-crypto/PROGRESS.md) · [Android Doze and App Standby](https://developer.android.com/training/monitoring-device-state/doze-standby)

Persist the reviewed bytes or digest, attempt ID, blockhash validity, returned signature and reconciliation reference. States include ready, wallet declined, submitted/pending, finalized, definitive failure and unknown. A callback wakes reconciliation and never serves as a receipt by itself. If a signature is missing after sign-and-send, search the transaction reference and verify exact content and execution before deciding what happened. Reopen a new review only after the earlier attempt is resolved or expired with adequate history coverage. Pocket spendable and incoming pending balances should never be added together as available funds.
