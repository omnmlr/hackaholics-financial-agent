# AGUS Crypto pre-PRD architecture decision

## Decision

**AGUS is an Android-first financial operating system for people who manage crypto across multiple existing wallets.** Start on Solana with Phantom and Solflare release candidates, then verify Backpack and additional wallets against the same adapter contract. Users should see one portfolio while retaining independent accounts and explicit control over which wallet spends. A person who connects existing wallets and never uses invoices or Pocket must still be able to understand, monitor and act on their finances.

| Priority | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| Core · Multi-wallet management | Combined balances, token and supported position coverage, account-level attribution, and activity with internal transfers identified where evidence supports it. |
| Core · Conversational financial intelligence | Ask about balances, changes, receipts, spending, or a specific transaction. Return answers with wallet, asset, time range, and inspectable evidence. |
| Core · Unified execution | Choose a funding wallet, review exact transfers or supported swaps, obtain the applicable authorization, and verify final settlement. |
| Core · Proactive assistance | Account activity monitoring, alerts, period summaries, budgets, and permission-aware suggestions. Users choose what they monitor and how often they hear from AGUS. |
| Major optional feature · AGUS Pocket | A separate Swig programmable account for active spending, in-app payments when the signer passes Android acceptance, and limited automation with explicit grants. |
| Supporting financial tools | Requests, invoices, links, QR codes, receipts, reconciliation and exports build on the same verified events. Useful across consumers, creators and teams. |

Use Android Mobile Wallet Adapter (MWA) for external-owner signing. Extend wallet-specific routes only after validating selection, callbacks and recovery. Keep Swig direct protocol as the preferred Pocket option. Offer payee-bound Solana Foundation Subscriptions as a separate opt-in for recurring pulls from an existing token account, subject to deployment and operational validation. These are three distinct signer/permission paths with one review and settlement contract. ## Android architecture and data boundaries

**Wallet registry.** A wallet record has a stable identity based on cluster and owner address. One wallet can have several connection routes, such as the same address linked twice, without adding to aggregate value. Label linked, watch-only and Pocket accounts and show signing capability separately from observation. Group user-designated own wallets apart from third-party watches. Verify the actual account returned by the signer at each handoff. Keep MWA credentials on the Android device and avoid importing recovery phrases or private keys.

**Portfolio and activity.** Ingest SOL balances and classic SPL/Token-2022 token accounts by mint and token program. Track positions through named protocol adapters and document supported coverage. A position counts once even if its receipt token is also visible in the wallet. Expose exact quantities, valuation timestamps, known priced subtotals and unsupported or stale data. Index both owner- and token-account activity, merge duplicates per transaction and transfer leg, and retain wallet attribution on both sides of confirmed internal transfers. Classify an internal transfer only when decoded endpoints map to the user's own accounts. Keep fees and other legs separate. Treat ambiguous, unknown and unsupported activity honestly. Avoid presenting an internal transfer as external earnings or spending. [Token account RPC](https://solana.com/docs/rpc/http/gettokenaccountsbyowner) · [Signature history RPC](https://solana.com/docs/rpc/http/getsignaturesforaddress) · [Transaction RPC](https://solana.com/docs/rpc/http/gettransaction)

**Conversation and proactive assistance.** Deterministic read tools calculate balances, time-windowed net flows, internal transfers, and available spending power. The model can clarify a question or draft an intent using those outputs. It returns source references and missing coverage instead of guessing at a value. A suggested source wallet includes the asset balance, SOL for fees, any account rent, supported route and the applicable authorization. The user selects the source. Independently of chat, an ingest worker evaluates opted-in account events and budgets, deduplicates alerts and links them back to transaction evidence. Add digests, appropriate privacy settings and eventually push delivery. The worker can observe and recommend without signing authority.

**Execution.** A wallet selector and deterministic composer turn a transfer or supported swap into an exact, decoded review. Display cluster, source wallet, recipient or destination account, asset and amount, fee payer, fees, relevant rent, and quote expiry. For a swap also validate quoted input/output, minimum receive, price impact, slippage, resolved address lookups and allowed programs. Pin the venue and route version for each adapter. Any relevant change invalidates the review. The user approves and the selected wallet signs, or a separately approved Pocket role signs within scope. Persist an attempt before opening a wallet. Check expected signature or reference, exact reviewed message, execution result, and finality before issuing a receipt. A lost callback means unknown until reconciliation. Never switch funding wallets or issue a replacement silently. [MWA signing](https://github.com/solana-mobile/mobile-wallet-adapter/blob/main/spec/spec.md#sign_and_send_transactions) · [Jupiter transaction building](https://developers.jup.ag/docs/swap/build)

**Optional extensions.** Pocket uses a user-held root and separate bounded device and worker roles. Root retains recovery, revocation and exit. The worker never receives an external wallet or root secret. Use the tested scope only, with a specific mint, allowed destination/program, cumulative budget and expiry. Calendar timing and app approval remain application controls. A cumulative slot window does not enforce a separate per-payment cap. Supporting tools use the same decoded events and settlement receipts. A Solana Pay request can add an opaque reference for matching, without putting invoice data on-chain or changing the wallet model. [Pocket feasibility](AGUS_POCKET_FEASIBILITY.md) · [Solana Pay](https://github.com/solana-foundation/pay)

## Contract handoff for parallel delivery

Use the existing typed Zod/OpenAPI layer in `packages/contracts` and generated `packages/api-client` for the server boundary. Reuse `AtomicMoney`, provenance and draft revisions where compatible. Keep the native signer adapter separate from those APIs. These shapes are proposed interface decisions for the next delivery phase. [Current shared contracts](https://github.com/omnmlr/hackaholics-financial-agent/blob/a26880a97faac0cde495e0f7f667ce86f3f8978a/packages/contracts/src/index.ts)

| Contract | Minimum stable shape | Responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `WalletRef` and `SigningRoute` | `walletId, cluster, ownerAddress, label, group, connectionKind`. Route reports provider, authorized address, supported operation and status. Groups distinguish own, watch and optional Pocket. | Mobile registers/refreshes available routes and holds device credentials. Backend stores authorized user scope and public address attribution, with no wallet auth token. |
| `PortfolioSnapshot` | `id, walletIds, observedAt, coverage[], assets[], positions[], pricedSubtotal`. Holdings include wallet, token-account, program/mint, atomic amount/decimals and optional timestamped valuation. Coverage names wallet, data kind, time window and ready/partial/stale/unavailable state. | Backend normalizes and deduplicates. Mobile groups and filters, displays stale/unpriced data without fabricating totals. |
| `ActivityPage` | Stable cursor and coverage, event ID, signature/slot/instruction path, link, per-leg signed atomic amounts and endpoint wallet IDs, finality, classification and reason. Use `internal`, `incoming`, `outgoing`, `swap` or `unknown` as applicable, with fee represented separately. | Backend classifies and merges observed legs. Mobile exposes wallet-level detail and verification links. |
| `Answer` and `Alert` | Answer includes structured amounts, snapshot ID/time bounds, event references and coverage limitations. Alert carries rule ID, dedupe key, event ID, matched account, status and timestamp. | Backend computes facts and creates deduped alerts. Mobile renders evidence and opens the associated activity. Chat proposes typed destinations/actions for review. |
| `ActionDraft` and native `SignerAdapter` | `id, revision, operation, cluster, sourceWalletId, routeId, asset, amount, destination, feePayer, fees, messageDigest, expiresAt`, plus serialized review message and optional quote. Native adapter accepts the reviewed message with expected account and returns declined, signature or unknown outcome. | Backend validates, composes, simulates and verifies. Mobile owns the exact approval/handoff. Changes to source, route, quote or bytes require a new review. |
| `ExecutionAttempt` and `Receipt` | Attempt records operation ID, review digest, expected cluster/account, signature or reference, and timestamps. States cover ready, handoff, declined, pending, unknown, failed, expired and finalized. Receipt records actual execution and finality evidence. | Persist before handoff. Reconcile a pending/unknown response across restart. Expiry plus adequate coverage must support any new approval. Evidence of final execution drives receipts and alerts. |

Amounts crossing the API are explicit decimal strings in atomic units with mint decimals. Times are UTC, and the reporting timezone is explicit when defining a day or month. IDs and classification enums stay stable throughout the demo. Provider, cluster, data scope and partial coverage are in the payload. Keep status/response fixtures versioned with the contract and generated client.

**Fixture acceptance for parallel work.** Use two owned Solana addresses, a duplicate route to the first address, and a watch-only address. Give the two owned addresses 100 and 25 units of the same USDC mint. A finalized 10-unit transfer between them changes the wallet balances to 90 and 35, with a combined 125 and zero external USDC flow. Keep its SOL fee as a separate debit. A later 5-unit outside inflow changes the combined total to 130 and creates one alert even when its event is delivered twice. Include an unsupported position, missing history, a wallet decline, a lost callback, and a restart after finalization. Frontend builds against these fixtures while backend integrates real reads and settlement. Both lanes run the same contract cases. Fixture activities and real network receipts must be labeled in the UI and demo.

## Release bounds

The November 29 slice covers two wallet views, a small asset set, grounded cross-wallet questions, an activity indication, and one safely verified signing route, with broader operation and two-wallet signing gates in the [readiness plan](HACKATHON_READINESS.md). Supported swaps, richer positions, deeper monitoring/budgets, additional wallets and networks, and Pocket remain part of the product direction. Enable live features only at the capability level each layer has verified.

A portfolio-only approach leaves the user assembling payments in other apps. A mandatory embedded wallet would remove the independent-custody design. Privy or Turnkey remain possible signer components for a future optional path given acceptance evidence. Squads remains a team option and LazorKit needs stronger audit and device evidence. For Pocket, prefer the tested Swig architecture and prove Android signing, root recovery, revocation and withdrawal before inviting real-funds use.
