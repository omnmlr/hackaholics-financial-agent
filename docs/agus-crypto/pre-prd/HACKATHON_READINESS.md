## Evidence threshold for 80/100

These artifacts would justify at least **16 usefulness + 12 innovation + 21 functionality + 11 mobile + 12 technical + 8 demo = 80** under this rubric. A screenshot, APK build or simulated callback alone does not satisfy the underlying behavior.

1. **Real two-wallet flow.** Release-signed Android APK links two distinct Devnet accounts, retains them after restart and shows one deduplicated portfolio. On the same device complete Phantom → Solflare → Phantom signing across payments/funding, with correctly selected payer and chain, verified app identity and three distinct finalized signatures with exact reviewed bytes. Keep Backpack labeled separately until tested.
2. **Invoice with a real receipt.** In Android create a USDC test invoice to a verified existing wallet, open/scan its link in a supported payer, settle once using a disposable account and show the verified destination, mint, amount, reference and signature. Inject or replay a duplicate event and prove one paid invoice. Show pending, wrong amount or wrong mint behavior and answer “Did James pay?” against the actual record with an inspectable source.
3. **Real optional Pocket.** Create and fund a separate Devnet Pocket with one reviewed external transfer. Pay a small test amount using a user-present or explicitly preauthorized Pocket role on the Android device. Show that wallet switching is absent for the chosen Pocket route. Demonstrate one out-of-policy refusal, final revocation, and root withdrawal to a verified external address. Show why a slot window is not a per-payment or calendar constraint. Record a recovery/exit drill after losing or clearing the app state.
4. **Reliable app state and demo.** Recover a pending or lost callback without an extra payment after process restart. Demonstrate rejection, correct return path, visible fee/approval count and one-context demo. Package an installable build and a short continuous recording with exact versions, source commit, expected cluster, timestamp, explorer receipts and spoken distinctions between local, Devnet and future claims.

The score is a planning judgment. If an external signer or in-app Pocket gate stays blocked, ship only the verified workflow and show the local Pocket experiment as separate evidence. A strong core-only path could approach the high 70s if its receipts, mobile recovery and presentation work. It should not borrow points from a simulated Android Pocket.

## Founder Android acceptance checklist

- Install the release-signed test APK, verify certificate, package, HTTPS identity and link association. Compare expected and actual wallet version, Android version, device and app return path. Phantom's warning is a blocker, even when a Digital Asset Links validator succeeds. Record logs surrounding the vendor force-close and check the chain before any repeat. Do not bypass the warning.
- Connect Phantom and Solflare with disposable Devnet accounts. Verify distinct stored grants and account/cluster on restart and wallet account changes. Repeat cold/warm. Perform the three signer-sequence operations in the 80-point gate and save each finalized reference.
- Toggle Android Battery Saver/Low Power Mode before the Solflare route and test an in-flight background/process kill and app return. Record actual wallet consent and unlock counts, p50/max handoff and completion time, whether the wallet showed a sheet/full-screen activity and any failed or ambiguous status. Aim for one wallet handoff per external transaction.
- Scan a Devnet invoice from a separate compatible test wallet, verify exact settled receipt, then reopen the app and find it via chat. Try a decline and a late callback. Confirm no false success, duplicate debit or duplicated invoice settlement.
- If Pocket is included, test device user presence, reject biometric/passkey cancellation and auth after backgrounding, fund from the intended wallet, pay from Pocket without reopening an external wallet, test revoke and root exit. Clear app state and recover through the designated user-controlled authority before calling it launch-ready.
