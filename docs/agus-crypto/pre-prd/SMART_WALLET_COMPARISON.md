## Selection

Use **MWA + Swig direct protocol** for a limited pilot. MWA routes existing-wallet funding and external direct payments. Swig owns Pocket permissions. An isolated signer holds only its scoped authority, and an external user-controlled root preserves an exit. Privy or Turnkey can later supply a human-authorized signer or a new separately funded wallet. If a provider signs a Swig role, on-chain Swig limits still apply to that role, and the provider joins the recovery and availability boundary. Combining two providers at launch would add two failure modes before either Android path is accepted.

For existing-wallet recurrence, retain **Subscriptions as a separate opt-in merchant route**. It can coexist with Pocket because its source is the opted-in canonical ATA. Confirm existing delegate conflicts and disclose what a grant authorizes. Ordinary payment transfer instructions are sufficient for funding and spending. A programmable account, a provider policy, and an escrow each solve different problems. The current evidence favors Swig for enforceable budgets and exit over a provider-only embedded wallet.
