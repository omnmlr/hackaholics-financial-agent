Two ways
to feel alive.

Two distinct interpretations of your financial companion. Both start with the same idea: your money deserves clarity, character and a little company. Explore the home and assistant experiences on desktop and mobile, then try the interactive prototypes.

Visual exploration only. The screens use an empty financial state and a clearly identified sample conversation.

What’s ready to explore

  • Home and assistant, independently composed for desktop and phone.
  • Five destinations, with conversations separate from activity.
  • Six expressive companion states and reduced-motion support.
  • A composer, saved-conversation access and payment-method discovery.

What the concepts don’t imply

Payments, uploads, speech and account connections are inactive. Pollar and Zcash are marked as proposed. This checkout currently uses Paystack. The mascot and wordmark are vector studies reconstructed from the supplied raster reference; use the original master artwork for production.
